# Media & Image Credits — NIGRANI

All background motifs, graphics, and visual elements within the NIGRANI frontend comply with the licensing and accessibility guidelines specified in `NIGRANI-FRONTEND-PLAN.md` §9 and `NIGRANI-I18N-AND-LAYOUT-PLAN.md` Part D.

## 1. Layer A — Geometric Motifs
- **Format**: Inline vector SVGs
- **License**: Dedicated to the Public Domain (CC0 1.0) by team GOD's Plan for SIH 2026.
- **Opacity**: Standardized at 4% (`0.04`) with a 240px vertical fade mask to ensure WCAG 2.1 AA text contrast compliance.
- **Motifs**:
  - `Ministry`: District-boundary lattice
  - `State`: Stacked horizontal register rules
  - `District`: Four-node ladder chain
  - `Member`: Rupee-column grid
  - `Case sheet`: Stepped ladder motif
  - `Rulebook`: Clause line blocks
  - `Alerts`: Diagonal chevron field
  - `Sign in`: Watermarked seal

## 2. Layer B — Architectural Hero Visuals & Carousel Assets
- **Location**: `frontend/src/assets/hero/hero-1.jpeg` through `hero-5.jpeg`
- **Subject**: Civic infrastructure & administrative public works — rural roads, school blocks, community halls, water storage structures, solar installations.
- **Source**: Public domain civic infrastructure photographic documentation (CC0 1.0 Universal).
- **Treatment**: Solid `rgba(7, 31, 54, 0.68)` scrim applied continuously over horizontal filmstrip slide to ensure ≥4.5:1 text contrast compliance for all white typography.
